Hi, I’m Rajni Pawar

I build systems for GPU data movement

I work with Dr. Xian-He Sun at the Gnosis Research Center on IOWarp, a data management platform for scientific and AI workloads. Scientific datasets now routinely outgrow GPU memory, and AI agents spend much of their effort just finding the right data. My research makes that data cheaper to move, store, and search.
